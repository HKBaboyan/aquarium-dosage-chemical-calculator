Understanding Aquarium Dosing

Precise dosing is vital for a healthy aquarium. While some chemicals have a wide safety margin, others can be toxic to fish or beneficial bacteria if accidentally overdosed.

The Manufacturer's Guide

This calculator provides a **general estimation** based on standard hobby concentrations. **Always prioritize the instructions printed on your specific product bottle.** Dosing can vary significantly between brands.

Key Tip for Success

  • Start Low: When introducing new fertilizers or medications, consider starting at 50% strength and observing your tank.
  • Overdosing Risks: Overdosing dechlorinator is usually safe, but overdosing medications or liquid carbon can stress or kill livestock.
  • Precision: Use a graduated syringe or a precise measuring spoon. Do not rely on "drops" or "capfuls" for sensitive dosages.
